I put together a study platform for your CAMS exam. Everything you need is in the attached zip. Takes 5 minutes to set up on your Mac.

## Setup

1. Download the attached `cams-study-guide.zip`
2. Double-click it to unzip. A folder called `cmas-study-guide` appears.
3. Move that folder to your Desktop
4. Drop your `CAMS Study Guide.pdf` into that folder (this unlocks clickable page links that open the original study guide side-by-side -- highly recommend)
5. Open the folder, go into `web`, double-click `start.command`
6. If your Mac blocks it: go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway"
7. A terminal window opens and your browser launches the study app. Keep that terminal window open while studying.

## What you get

- **Study Plan** -- 31-day plan starting now through May 1. Checkboxes save automatically. Complete a day and you get a surprise.
- **5 Study Guides** -- All 4 exam domains + FATF reference, condensed from the 400+ page PDF. Every section has clickable page badges -- click one and the original PDF opens side-by-side so you can read the full source without losing your place.
- **Glossary** -- 108 key terms, 57 acronyms, all 40 FATF recommendations. Searchable, filterable by domain.
- **Flashcards** -- Drill vocabulary. Tracks what you know vs. need to review.
- **Dark mode** -- Moon icon, top right.

## Quick exam facts

- 120 multiple choice, 3.5 hours, need ~75 correct (62.5%)
- Domain III (Compliance Programs) = 28% -- study this the most
- Red flags = most tested section -- know these cold
- Understand concepts, don't memorize -- the exam tests scenarios

## Daily routine

1. Open the Study Plan, check today's tasks
2. Read the assigned guide sections
3. Click the page badges to cross-reference the original PDF
4. Drill 15 min of flashcards
5. Check off your tasks
